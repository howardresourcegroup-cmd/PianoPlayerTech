5. About Tuning & Tuning Stability

Please understand how piano tuning works before booking:

A piano tuning is not guaranteed to "stay in tune" for any specific length of time. Once a piano is brought to pitch, its tuning is affected by changes in humidity and temperature in your home — conditions we cannot control after we leave. This is normal for every piano and every technician.
  • Pianos remain most stable in a relatively steady environment (industry guidance is roughly 35–50% relative humidity).
  • A piano that is significantly flat, or hasn't been tuned in a long time, may require a pitch raise and may need to be tuned more than once — sometimes in the same visit — to hold a stable pitch. This additional work may add to the cost, and we will tell you if it's needed.
  • New pianos and recently moved pianos typically need extra tunings as they settle.
