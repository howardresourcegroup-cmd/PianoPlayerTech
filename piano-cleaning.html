Why It Matters

More Than Looks

A clean piano isn't just about appearance. Dust works its way into the action and around the soundboard over the years, and the wrong cleaning products can permanently damage a finish or the keys. We clean the way a piano should be cleaned — carefully, by hand, with materials made for instruments. It's a natural pairing with a tuning visit, and we're happy to do both in one trip.

FAQ

Piano Cleaning Questions

What does piano cleaning include?
We clean the exterior case and finish, the keys, and the accessible interior — removing dust, grime, and buildup that collects over years. Detailing can also include polishing the case, cleaning under the keys, and clearing dust from the soundboard and action area. We never use household cleaners that can harm a piano's finish.
Is it safe to clean a piano myself?
Light dusting with a soft cloth is fine, but household sprays, polishes, and water can damage the finish, keys, and internal felts. Deep cleaning and detailing should be done by hand with the right materials — that's what we do, in your home.
Do you clean the inside of the piano?
Yes. We carefully clear dust and debris from the accessible interior — around the action, strings, and soundboard. This is delicate work; we don't disturb the tuning or moving parts.
How much does piano cleaning cost?
Every piano is different, so cleaning and detailing are quote-based. Tell us your piano's make, type, and condition and we'll give you a price. We can often combine cleaning with a tuning visit to save you a trip.
Do you come to us?
Yes. All cleaning and detailing is done in-home throughout metro Atlanta and North Georgia. There's no need to move your piano.
