What Detailing Covers

Down to the Details

Pianos are delicate, and decades of dust work their way into places a wipe-down never reaches. Detailing addresses all of it — carefully.

Cabinet & Finish

The case is cleaned, hand-polished, and refreshed appropriately for its finish — fingerprints, haze, and built-up grime removed.

Keys & Keybed

Every key detailed top, sides, and between, plus the dust and debris that collects beneath them along the keybed.

Interior & Soundboard

Dust and debris cleared from the accessible interior — around the action, strings, and soundboard — without disturbing the tuning or moving parts.

Hardware & Brass

Pedals, hinges, casters, and brass cleaned so the details look as cared-for as the rest of the instrument.

Why It Matters

More Than Looks

A detailed piano isn't just about appearance. Dust works its way into the action and around the soundboard over the years, and the wrong cleaning products can permanently damage a finish or the keys. Detailing is done the way a fine instrument should be cared for — carefully, by hand, with materials made for pianos. It's the perfect prep before selling, a fresh start for an inherited piano, and a natural pairing with a tuning. We're happy to do both in one trip.

FAQ

Piano Detailing Questions

What's the difference between cleaning and detailing?
Cleaning is standard upkeep — the exterior case, keys, and a light interior dusting. Detailing is the premium, restoration-grade treatment: everything in a cleaning plus thorough interior work around the action and soundboard, hardware and brass, keytop detailing, and a careful cabinet finish polish. It's the deep clean a piano gets before a sale, after an estate, or when it simply hasn't been touched in years.
What does piano detailing include?
A full hand detail of the case and finish, every key cleaned top and sides, dust and debris cleared from the accessible interior around the action, strings, and soundboard, hardware and brass cleaned, and the cabinet polished appropriately for its finish. We never use household sprays or polishes that can harm a piano.
How much does piano detailing cost?
Piano detailing is $175 per piano, and standard piano cleaning is $125 per piano. If you have more than one piano, ask about our multi-piano discounts. You can also bundle detailing with a tuning or player repair in the same visit and save on the total.
Will detailing affect my piano's tuning?
No. Detailing is careful, by-hand work and we don't disturb the tuning or moving parts. If your piano is due for a tuning, we're happy to do both in one visit.
Do you detail the piano in my home?
Yes. All detailing is done in-home throughout metro Atlanta and North Georgia. There's no need to move your piano.
Can I bundle detailing with a tuning or player repair?
Yes — and you'll save. Since we're already coming to you, adding a tuning or player-piano repair to your cleaning or detailing visit earns a discount off the total. Just let us know what else your piano needs and we'll price it together.
