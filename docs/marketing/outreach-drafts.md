## Note to piano tuners and technicians

Subject: Somewhere to send the player system jobs

Hi [Name],

I run PianoPlayerTech. We work on player systems: Disklavier, PianoDisc, QRS
and Spirio on the electronic side, and antique pneumatic players on the other.

Player systems sit in an awkward gap. They are too electronic for most piano
technicians and too piano-specific for most electronics shops. If you tune a
piano and the customer mentions the player has stopped working, we can take
that part.

Two ways it can work:

- You keep the customer. Pull the power supply or driver board, ship it to us,
  and reinstall it when it comes back. We bench-test it and quote in writing
  before any work. If we cannot repair it, you pay return shipping only.
- You refer the customer. In metro Atlanta and North Georgia we come to the
