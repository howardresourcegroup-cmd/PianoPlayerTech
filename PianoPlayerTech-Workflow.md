# PianoPlayerTech — How It All Works

A plain walkthrough of a job from first contact to getting paid. Keep this and the contract + payout log together.

## Who does what
- **Ethan** (Howard Resource Group LLC, d/b/a PianoPlayerTech) — owns the brand, website, Stripe, phone/email; does **player-piano repair**; handles payouts, books, and taxes.
- **Christian** (1099 contractor) — does **tuning & voicing**; runs his own tuning calendar.
- **Both** — can do **piano detailing**.

---

## The flow, step by step

### 1. A lead comes in
A customer finds us through Google (the Business Profile), the website, a referral, or a piano teacher. They reach us by:
- **Booking a tuning time** on Christian's Google Calendar (the "Book a Time" button) — they pick a slot.
- **"Book & Pay" form** on the tuning page — sends their details and takes payment ($175, or $125/piano bulk).
- **Player-repair intake form** — sends details and takes the $100 diagnosis payment.
- **Phone** (470) 758-9572 or **email** info@pianoplayertech.com.
- **Teacher partner form** (the /piano-teachers page).

**Every form submission emails `pianoplayertech@gmail.com`** (via Formspree). All `@pianoplayertech.com` email forwards to that inbox too.

### 2. Confirm the appointment
- Check the inbox + Christian's calendar **daily**.
- Confirm timing with the customer within **one business day** (that's what the forms promise).
- Tuning/voicing → **Christian**. Player repair → **Ethan**. Both on one visit → coordinate.

### 3. Payment
- All payment runs through the **PPT Stripe account** (live). It happens at booking (the forms) — or send a Stripe link / collect at the visit when needed.
- Money lands in Stripe → your bank.

### 4. Do the work
- Perform the service. Honest assessment, no high-pressure upselling — that's the brand.

### 5. Log the job
- Open **PianoPlayerTech-Payout-Log.csv**. Add one row: Date, Customer, Service, **Worked By** (Christian / Both / Ethan), Gross $.
- The sheet auto-calculates the Stripe fee, net, Christian's cut, and the company's cut.

### 6. Ask for the review (right after the job)
- Christian shows the **QR code at /review** on his phone, or sends the post-job text:
  > "Thanks again for having PianoPlayerTech out today! If you were happy with the tuning, a quick Google review really helps — about 30 seconds: pianoplayertech.com/review"

### 7. Pay Christian
- **Weekly, by Zelle.** The payout log tells you exactly what he's owed.

### 8. Money & taxes (ongoing)
- Get Christian's **W-9** before his first payout.
- Keep PPT money in **one account**.
- **January:** issue Christian a **1099-NEC** if you paid him $600+ that year (the payout log's TOTALS row is his number).

- PPT job, **both of you** → 50 / 50
- PPT job, **Ethan alone** (player repair) → 100% Company
- Customer **Christian found himself** → 100% his, not logged here
