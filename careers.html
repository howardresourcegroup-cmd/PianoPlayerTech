Lead Responder

You are the first voice a customer hears. You answer inbound calls and website form leads within 5 minutes, find out what the piano is and what it needs, quote from our price sheet, book the visit on the technicians’ calendars, and follow up with anyone who has not booked yet. All of it happens in our lead system, from wherever you work.

Pay
$15–20 an hour
Schedule
About 5 hours a week, remote. The work comes in as calls and form leads, so being reachable matters more than set hours.
Reports to
Ethan, the owner
Training
Training on our price sheet, the lead system and the technicians’ calendars before you take your first call.

Part-time · In-home visits

Player System Apprentice

You go on in-home visits with our lead technician and learn to diagnose Yamaha Disklavier, PianoDisc, QRS and Steinway Spirio systems: power supplies, driver boards, solenoids and the rest. An electronics or electrical engineering background is preferred, because most of the work is at the board level.

Pay
$18–22 an hour
Schedule
Part-time, on in-home visits alongside the lead technician.
Reports to
The lead technician
Training
Training provided. You learn Disklavier, PianoDisc, QRS and Spirio diagnostics hands-on, on real service calls.

Work With Us

Join Our Team

Player piano technicians are rare. We’re building a team across North Georgia and looking for people who take craftsmanship seriously — whether you have years of experience or just a strong mechanical aptitude and a willingness to learn.

What the Work Looks Like

  • In-home service visits throughout North Georgia
  • Diagnosing and rebuilding pneumatic player systems
  • Sourcing and fitting period-correct parts
  • Tuning and regulating acoustic pianos
  • Working directly with customers — explaining what you found and what it costs

Who We’re Looking For

  • Experienced piano or player piano technicians
  • RPTs (Registered Piano Technicians) open to player system work
  • Mechanically skilled people with patience for detailed work
  • Apprentices who want to learn a rare and in-demand trade
  • Self-starters who can manage their own schedule

What We Offer

  • Flexible scheduling — work fits around your life
  • Training provided for the right candidate
  • Growing market with very little local competition
  • Backed by Howard Resource Group’s established client base
  • Pay stated up front for every open role
