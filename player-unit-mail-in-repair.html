Why Repair

These Units Aren’t Made Any More

The replacement market for legacy player electronics is thin, expensive, and full of units the same age as the one that just died.

Discontinued Means Used-Market Only

A PianoCD or PDS-128 control box hasn’t been manufactured in years. eBay is the market, and every unit on it carries the same aging capacitors as the one you are replacing.

The Failure Is Usually Components, Not the Board

Electrolytic capacitors, regulators and relays are what age out. The board itself is generally fine. Replacing those parts restores the unit you already own.

Player Technicians Are Rare

Player systems sit in an awkward gap: too electronic for most piano technicians, too piano-specific for most electronics shops. Shipping the unit puts it on a bench that handles these systems.

Upgrades Aren’t Always the Answer

PianoDisc’s official path for a dead legacy box is an iQ upgrade — a good option, but not what every owner wants or can afford. Repair keeps the original system playing.
