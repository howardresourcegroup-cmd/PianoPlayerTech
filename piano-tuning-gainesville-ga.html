FAQ

Gainesville Piano Tuning Questions

How much does piano tuning cost in Gainesville?
$175 flat per piano, travel included throughout Hall County. Four or more pianos tuned in one visit — common for churches and schools — drops the rate to $125 per piano.
Do you cover Flowery Branch, Buford, and Braselton?
Yes — all of Hall County and the south-county corridor: Gainesville, Oakwood, Flowery Branch, Lula, Clermont, Buford, and Braselton. The Lake Lanier communities are all on our regular route.
Why does my piano go out of tune so fast near the lake?
Humidity. Pianos are wood, and the seasonal moisture swings around Lake Lanier expand and contract the soundboard, which changes string tension. Twice-yearly tuning is the realistic schedule here; a humidity-control system can stretch that if your piano struggles.
How long does a tuning take?
Most tunings take 60 to 90 minutes. A piano that hasn't been serviced in years may need a pitch raise first — Christian will assess on-site and tell you before any extra work happens.
How do I book?
Book and pay online — pick your preferred times, pay the $175, and Christian confirms your exact appointment within 24 hours. Or call (470) 758-9572.
