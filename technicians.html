Piano Tuning & Voicing

Christian handles all piano tuning and voicing throughout North Georgia. Whether you need a routine tune-up, a pitch raise after years of neglect, or voicing work to brighten or mellow your piano's tone, Christian brings a careful, unhurried approach to every visit.

He works with all makes and sizes — upright studio pianos to concert grands — and offers bulk rates for churches, schools, and studios with four or more instruments.

Ethan Howard
Player Piano Technician & Owner
Player Piano Repair & Restoration

Ethan founded Piano Player Tech to bring specialized player piano repair back to North Georgia — a craft that few shops still practice. He handles everything from a stuck roll motor to a full pneumatic rebuild: valve work, bellows replacement, tracker bars, and the mechanical linkages that make a player system perform the way it was designed to.

All work starts with a $100 flat-rate in-home diagnosis so you know exactly what you're dealing with before any labor begins. If we can't fix it, you don't pay for the repair.
