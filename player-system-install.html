FAQ

Install & Removal Questions

Can you add a player system to the piano I already own?
Yes. We install PianoDisc and QRS systems on existing pianos. Send the make and model of the piano, with photos if you can, and we tell you what fits.
Do you remove Disklavier systems?
Yes. We remove Yamaha Disklavier systems. Tell us the model and we quote the removal in writing.
How is an install or removal priced?
Every job is quoted in writing after photos or a visit, before any work starts. Pianos and systems vary too much to quote sight-unseen.
I’m a dealer. Do you do labor-only work?
Yes. Dealers: we do labor-only installs and removals. When you supply the system, you pay for the labor only. Send the model and we quote in writing.
My player system is installed but not working. Is this the right page?
For a system that has stopped working, see player piano repair — that starts with the $100 flat-rate in-home diagnosis.
