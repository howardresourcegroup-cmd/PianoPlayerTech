What’s Included

Tone, Touch and Evenness

Tuning brings a piano to pitch. Voicing and regulation are what make it sound and feel right under the hands.

Hammer Voicing

The hammer felts are worked to even out the tone note to note — taking the edge off a bright, clangy piano or bringing life back to a dull one.

Action Regulation

Keys, hammers, dampers and the parts between them adjusted so every note repeats cleanly and the action responds the way it should.

Touch-Weight

How heavy the keys feel, and how evenly, measured and brought back into balance across the keyboard.

FAQ

Voicing & Regulation Questions

What is the difference between tuning and voicing?
Tuning sets the pitch of every string. Voicing shapes the tone, by working the felt of the hammers. A piano can be perfectly in tune and still sound harsh, thin or dull — that is a voicing job.
What is regulation?
Regulation is the adjustment of the action: the keys, hammers, dampers and the many small parts between them, so every note repeats cleanly and feels the same under the finger.
What is touch-weight?
Touch-weight is how heavy or light the keys feel to play, and how even that feel is from one end of the keyboard to the other. It can be measured and brought back into balance.
How much does voicing and regulation cost?
It is quoted in writing after a visit. How much work a piano needs depends on its age, how much it is played and how it was last set up, and that can only be judged at the piano.

Can I book it with a tuning?
