FAQ

Piano Detailing Questions

How is detailing different from piano cleaning?
A piano cleaning is routine upkeep. Detailing is restoration-grade: every surface of the case, every key, the hardware and the accessible interior are worked by hand, the way a piano is prepared before a sale, after an estate, or after years untouched.
How much does piano detailing cost?
Piano detailing is $175 per piano, done in your home. Add a tuning or a repair to the same visit and a bundle discount comes off the total.
Is there a discount if I add a tuning or repair?
Yes. Add a tuning or a repair to the detailing visit and the bundle discount comes off the total. We confirm it when we book the visit.
Do you detail the piano in my home?
Yes. Detailing is done in-home across metro Atlanta and North Georgia. There is no need to move the piano.
Will detailing affect my piano’s tuning?
No. Detailing is careful, by-hand work and does not disturb the tuning or the moving parts. If the piano is due for a tuning, add it to the same visit.
What do you clean it with?
Materials made for pianos. Never household sprays or polishes, which can permanently damage a finish or the keys.
