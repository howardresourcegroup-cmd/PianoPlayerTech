Why Refurbish

These Units Aren’t Made Any More

The replacement market for legacy player electronics is thin, expensive, and full of boards the same age as the one that just died.

01

Discontinued Means Used-Market Only

A PianoCD or PDS-128 control box hasn’t been manufactured in years. eBay is the market, and every unit on it carries the same aging capacitors as the one you are replacing.

02

The Failure Is Usually Components, Not the Board

Electrolytic capacitors, regulators and relays are what age out. The board itself is generally fine. Replacing those parts restores the unit your customer already owns.

03

You Don’t Have to Take On Board-Level Work

Plenty of excellent piano technicians have no interest in surface-mount rework, and shouldn’t need one. Diagnose the system, pull the unit, ship it, reinstall it. We handle the electronics.

04

Upgrades Aren’t Always the Answer

PianoDisc’s official path for a dead legacy box is an iQ upgrade — a good option, but not what every customer wants or can afford. Refurbishment keeps the original system playing.

FAQ

Trade Service Questions

Do you accept boards shipped from outside Georgia?
Yes. Bench work is not geographically limited — technicians anywhere in the country can ship a power supply or driver board to us. In-home service is metro Atlanta and North Georgia only, but refurbishment work travels.
What do you refurbish?
PianoDisc power supplies including PDS-128 and 228CFX, keydriver and solenoid driver boards, Disklavier power supplies across generations, QRS Pianomation boards, and pneumatic components from antique players. If you are not sure whether a unit is worth saving, send a photo before you ship it.
Why refurbish instead of replacing the unit?
Most of these units are discontinued. A PianoCD or PDS-128 control box is no longer manufactured, so the used market is the only source for a like-for-like replacement — and those units carry the same aging components. Component-level repair restores the board you already have rather than gambling on another twenty-year-old one.
How does the process work?
Contact us first so we know what is coming and can confirm the unit is worth doing. Ship it in. We bench-test it, identify what has failed, and send you a written quote before any work starts. Once approved we refurbish and return it. If we cannot repair it, you pay only return shipping.
Do you work with dealers and rebuilders?
Yes. Technicians, rebuilding shops and dealers are the bulk of this side of the business. If you handle player systems occasionally but do not want to take on board-level electronics, this is what we are here for.
Can you help if I’m stuck mid-diagnosis?
Call. If we can save you a shipping label by talking it through, we will — a surprising number of player system faults are power, connector, or configuration issues rather than board failures.
